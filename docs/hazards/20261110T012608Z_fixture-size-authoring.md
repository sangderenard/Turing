# Fixture dimensions and kicker snoot authoring

Adds a lighting-profile fixtureSizes field and isolated marble_fixture_sizes.js controller. New rectangular fixtures (both keys, lower/upper fill, gridded underlight) start with width/height factors 2. Round fixture diameter remains factor1 and is adjustable. Missing legacy profile dimensions migrate to factor1 without reauthoring saved geometry. The knobs are dimension multipliers of the incumbent role dimensions, including the key-shape-dependent baseline; they are not absolute millimetres. Bounds are 0.1–10. Round lights have one diameter factor, rectangular lights independent width/height.
