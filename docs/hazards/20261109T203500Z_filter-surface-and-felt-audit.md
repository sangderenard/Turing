## Focused opaque diffuse correction

After the audit, the actual forward photon and reactive paths and image SensorAccumulator shader gained a narrow fully diffuse opaque/nonemissive branch. It uses the existing authored RGB spectral approximation as boundary reflectance, rather than the specular-style compatibility scalar and .02 floor. Sensor direct/field terms already multiply spectral surface color, so the scalar is1 there (not a second tint). Camera continuation multiplies each channel's reflectance divided by a separate roulette survival probability; forward packets multiply their own wavelength reflectance once. This matte route does not apply the legacy distance-based opaque absorption/falloff at the boundary. Glossy, metal, glass and explicit medium interfaces are unchanged. Native material tables and scene units are unchanged.
