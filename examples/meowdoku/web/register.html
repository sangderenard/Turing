What sits behind this page

The visible front end was assembled in about 20 minutes. It opens a window onto roughly four years of backend development. SpeakToMe began as a way to explore branching language-model possibilities. The surrounding work grew into separate systems for graph execution, tensor and compiler research, managed scientific time, geometry, audio, optics, and simulation. Some of those systems are demonstrated here; an isolated demo does not mean every part is integrated into one finished product.

Alpha participants help expose the seams between those systems: where an interface is unclear, a computation fails, a result needs verification, or a research idea meets real use. Activity and feedback can inform debugging, evaluation, and training. Expect experiments and revisions rather than a polished service contract.
