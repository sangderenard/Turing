Source divider

Give the divider a Python source and it becomes one job, divided into pieces. Pieces are tried one at a time, dependencies first, on each chosen backend.

The queue is one piece at a time: a job can wait behind other pieces. Repo links are recorded but not fetched yet; this server makes no outbound request, so a linked job stays awaiting_fetch. Upload a .py file (up to 512 KB) or a .zip (up to 20 MB) to start a job now.
