Record a new animation

Recording runs the game’s own fountain in the preview. Each flip moves the case and its grains about the case normal; the law applies the corresponding turn forces. The spring contact limit can lengthen the requested flip. For large simulations, only sampled visual frames are kept; the saved animation holds at most 1,024 grains and 1 MiB of raw positions. A save needs owner activation before players see it.
