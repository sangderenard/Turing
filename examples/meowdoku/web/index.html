meowdoku: pick Cats or X marks, then tap; swipe paints X marks, double-tap is always a cat; tap the cat, mouse or bulb under the board to use a power-up · chess/checkers: tap a piece, then a lit square · wordle: type or tap the rack · space_invaders: arrows or A/D move, space fires; on a phone hold a finger where the rocket should go · bullet_hell: arrows or WASD fly, space fires; on a phone hold a finger below the ship · tilequest: arrows or WASD walk, space swings the sword; villagers commute, towers shoot monsters · boardwalk: turn race round a forking track; on your turn left/right picks the fork, up runs, down rests, space rolls and goes · worldwalk: you are a wizard on foot, not a rocket: tap the globe to walk there along the cheapest path (roads are fastest, water a slow swim), or A/D turn and W/S walk (let go and you stop; any key cancels the walk), space swings the sword; the bar's tap menu turns a tap into a house, wall, tower or market placed beside a road, Build here places one near you; settlers commute along the roads, builders found lights, towers shoot raiders · purrline: match three; tap a tile then a neighbour (or drag it) to swap; 4 in a row makes a pounce (row + column), an L or T a hairball (3x3), 5 a catnip (swap it with any tile to clear that kind); cascades multiply the score · delve: a map of rooms; A/D or arrows run, Space (or W/Up) jumps (hold for height), W/S climb ladders, walk off the edge of a room through a door or down a ladder shaft to the next one, M shows the map; the double-jump orb opens the high ledges · worldwalk: A/D turn, W/S throttle (as bullet_hell), space swings the sword; settlers commute along the roads, builders found lights, towers shoot raiders
