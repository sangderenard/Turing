Marble size & physical properties

Size changes keep the material definition and table contact. Thickness, absorption, mass and inertia change physically. User orbit stays centred on the displayed marble without changing zoom. A held photographic pose stays fixed. Particle-course radius remains a separate simulator setting.

Contact frequency controls the soft-sphere spring: wall stiffness is particle mass × frequency², and particle-pair stiffness uses reduced mass. These are relative effective DEM presets, not measured Young moduli. The 1/360 s explicit solver caps frequency at 200; literal glass, metal and ceramic contact stiffness would require a much smaller step or another contact model. Changing contact settings restarts the particle preview.
