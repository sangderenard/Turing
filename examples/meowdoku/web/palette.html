Play the color preference game

Each round shows designs drawn at random, each a small page: a palette of one to six named colors, and how every part of the page wears them (nav bar, header, cards, text, links, inputs, buttons, badges), with its own corners, borders, bevels and shadows. Choose them favorite first. A learning network, compiled to native code, proposes a design for your view of the site; score it with the slider. Everyone's answers teach the shared network, which is the site's default. Your own answers also teach a small personal layer that nudges that default for you alone.
